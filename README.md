# **PC Data Sales**

Analysing PC sales to identify trends, understand customer demand and support better business decisions. 


## **Table of Contents**
- Description
- Project flow
- Model diagram

## **Description**
In this project, we processed previous PC sales to identify factors such as top-performing products, seasonal fluctuations, regional performance differences and customer purchasing behaviour. 

The goal of the project it to transform raw data into meaningful insights through data cleaning, exploration and visualization. This enables stakeholders and businesses to idnetify key factors influencing sales performance and refine their marketing strategies.
